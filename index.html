Welcome to Mini Market

This is a mini market game
Learn how markets move, buy & sell companies, and watch your net worth change — no real money used.
Patch notes & Quick Notes
  • Game length is fixed to 365 days initially; progress won't reset when you extend the simulation.
  • When 365 days finish you'll be asked if you want to continue — extend to 700 days without resetting the day count.
  • Click a big category button to view that category's 10 stocks. There is no "All" view.
  • After each week you first see your growth then the upcoming news with simple explanations.
  • Speed button applies immediately (1×, 2×, 4×). Days now run slower so it's easier to read popups.
